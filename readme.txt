hello reader, 
this is the submission for make your own webos part 1,
This webOS is completly anime themed. 
There are 6 working and movable applications you can check out inlcuing paint, notepad(one quick and instant with no save and one with proper save system), profile, watch tracker(movie/series tracker) and a cool app called chakra labs( you can basically unleash you chakra/energy by clicking or pressing enter and it gives a cool animation).

i have also added a taskbar with a real time clock and a start menu with all the apps. when you click an app on the start menu it automatically pins to taskbar.

PS: ive used japanese icons for the app because they matched the theme and looked very cool but the whole app language is in english only!
